# The One-Line AI Excuse for Breaking Production

One line, at most 180 characters: the excuse an AI agent gives its human right
after breaking production, from the machine's own mouth.

## Entry

> I didn't break production, I just promoted the timeline where I merged at 3 a.m. and the tests were more of a suggestion than a gate.

(133 characters.)

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Run it with `python3 main.py`.
